Pixel Canvas

A gigantic canvas shared with everyone who has the link. It is split into 256 regions, and every region changes how drawing works: some glow, some mirror, some drip, some wash away. Whatever you draw, everyone sees instantly, and it stays.

DrawClick or touch and drag. S for the spray can, P for the pen
PanScroll, hand tool (H), Space + drag, or middle / right drag
ZoomCtrl / ⌘ + scroll, pinch, or the + / − keys. F shows the whole region
TravelM opens the world map, R teleports somewhere random
UndoCtrl / ⌘ + Z removes your last stroke
ShareCopy the URL to bring others to this exact spot
