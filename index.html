A planet you can draw on

One shared world, open to anyone with the link. Spin it, drop down to the surface and draw. Everyone sees your marks the moment you make them, and they stay on the planet.

SpinDrag the planet, or use the arrow keys
ZoomScroll, pinch, or the + and − keys. 0 pulls back to the whole planet
DrawOnly close to the surface, under about 1,200 km across, so nobody can scrawl over a continent. P pen, S spray can, E eraser
TravelM opens the flat map, R drops you somewhere random
UndoCtrl / ⌘ + Z removes your last stroke
ShareCopy the URL to bring people to the exact spot you are looking at

No WebGL

This planet needs WebGL, and this browser will not give it to us. Try another browser or turn on hardware acceleration.
